Monsoon arrives early

The southwest monsoon reached the coast three days ahead of schedule, the weather office said.

What to expect

Heavy rain is likely in these districts:

  • Coastal areas

    • low-lying wards

    • river banks

  • Hill stations

Steps for residents

  1. Keep drains clear.

  2. Store drinking water.

We are ready, said the commissioner.
